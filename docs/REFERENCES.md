# References & Bibliography

1. **StrokeRehab Primary Dataset**: A. Kaku, A. Parnandi, A. Khemani et al., "StrokeRehab: A Benchmark Dataset for Sub-second Action Quality Assessment," *Machine Learning for Healthcare (MLHC)* / *NeurIPS Track on Datasets and Benchmarks*, 2020 / 2022. Official portal: `https://strokerehabdata.github.io/`.
2. **PhysioNet Multi-Camera Gait Reference**: A. Goldberger et al., "PhysioBank, PhysioToolkit, and PhysioNet: Components of a new research resource for complex physiologic signals," *Circulation*, vol. 101, no. 23, pp. e215-e220, 2000. Official dataset: `https://physionet.org/content/multi-gait-posture/1.0.0/`.
3. **MediaPipe Pose Tracking**: C. Lugaresi et al., "MediaPipe: A Framework for Building Perception Pipelines," *arXiv preprint arXiv:1906.08172*, 2019.
4. **Fugl-Meyer Assessment**: A. R. Fugl-Meyer et al., "The post-stroke hemiplegic patient. 1. a method for evaluation of physical performance," *Scandinavian Journal of Rehabilitation Medicine*, vol. 7, no. 1, pp. 13-31, 1975.
5. **Berg Balance Scale**: K. O. Berg et al., "Measuring balance in the elderly: validation of an instrument," *Canadian Journal of Public Health*, vol. 83, pp. S7-11, 1992.
6. **GroupKFold Subject-Independent Validation**: F. Pedregosa et al., "Scikit-learn: Machine learning in Python," *Journal of Machine Learning Research*, vol. 12, pp. 2825-2830, 2011.
7. **Stroke Rehabilitation Biomechanics**: D. A. Winter, *Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement*, 4th ed., John Wiley & Sons, 2009.
